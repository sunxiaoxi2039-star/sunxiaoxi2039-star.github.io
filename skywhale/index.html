SKY WHALE

A 100-second anime short, drawn and animated entirely in code. One day, dawn to night.

1920×1080, 48 fps. If the video is missing, open the full film as a live, self-contained page: scenes/film.html.

Five movements

  1. Dawn: The Breakout. The cloud sea swells, domes and tears as the Old Whale rises through it and breaches into the golden sky.
  2. Morning: The Crossing. Over a patchwork valley a kilometre below, a great junk-sail fin strokes, and a cream cloud bank swallows the whale.
  3. Storm: The Squall. Rain and lightning, then the whale bursts out of the storm top into sunlight under a rainbow.
  4. Dusk: The Calling. Over a calm sea it breathes, blows and calls. A hard cut on the silence, and no answer comes.
  5. Night: The Answer. Under the moon, warm eyes appear along the cloud band, and the pod rises to meet it.

How it is made

Every frame renders deterministically to a WebGL2 canvas from the code in src/. There are no images, footage or AI-generated assets. The whale is an analytic body whose plates, rivets, rust, keel planking, moss, brass eye and ink lines are computed per pixel from stable body coordinates, so the line work never boils. The clouds are painted puffs pushed around by a simulation in which each whale is a moving solid. The soundtrack is an orchestral score written as code and played on real sampled instruments, with whale voices built from real humpback recordings.
